A home widget for the Hildon Home (the Maemo Leste desktop): a calendar sheet with the month, the day of the month and the weekday, following the system date.

It needs the Hildon package; Catalogue installs it first. To show it, open Home (Hildon) from the menu, hold a finger on the Home until edit mode starts, tap Add widget and choose Date. In edit mode a widget can be dragged to another place.
